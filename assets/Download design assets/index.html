Let’s talk
Is the planner really free?

Yes. Free includes your budget, checklist, vendors, payments, a guest list for up to 100 people and a branded wedding website. Premium adds the guest-facing extras and higher limits.

Can we plan together?

Yes. Invite your partner so you can work on your plans together and keep your decisions in the same place.

How do invitations and RSVPs work?

With Premium, choose a matching collection or create your own designs. Publish each piece when ready, share a wedding link or QR, and send personal guest messages. Invitation and website replies connect to the same guest list in Share & RSVPs.

What if we already have a spreadsheet?

You can bring your guest list across with CSV import. Then use the planner to keep your budget, tasks and guest details together.

Does it work outside Australia?

Yes. The planner supports weddings around the world, with deeper localisation for Australia, New Zealand, the UK, the US and Canada. You can choose your currency above.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is one payment for your wedding. There is no recurring subscription to renew or cancel.
